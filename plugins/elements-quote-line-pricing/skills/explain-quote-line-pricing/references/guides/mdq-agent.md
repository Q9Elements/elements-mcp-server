# MDQ Agent (Multi-Dimensional Quoting) guide

Multi-Dimensional Quoting (MDQ) divides a subscription into separately priced segments. Read `flags.isMdq`, `mdq`, `proration`, `quote.firstSegmentTermEndDate`, `line`, and `priceWaterfall.stages`. A confirmed non-MDQ line is not applicable; a missing or unreadable MDQ section for a line identified as MDQ is a gap. Flags can be false when inputs are unreadable.

Use `mdq.currentSegmentIndex`, `currentSegmentLabel`, `currentDimensionType`, and `segments[].isCurrent` to identify the current segment. Sort segments by index. `mdq.grouping` says whether segments are grouped by segment key or dimension; dimension grouping can omit other dimensions, including one-time charges. `summary.hasDuplicateIndex` signals ambiguity: do not present an unqualified segment comparison or rollup. Use `summary` totals only with its grouping and coverage limits.

Segment `listPrice`, `specialPrice`, `proratedPrice`, `regularPrice`, `customerPrice`, and `netPrice` are unit prices; `listTotal`, `customerTotal`, and `netTotal` are extended totals. Do not chain a total with a unit price. The top-level waterfall is authoritative for the current segment. Use recorded quantity and totals, and do not assume that a missing total is zero.

Uplift compounds from the previous segment's Regular unit price, excluding manually entered additional discounts; the first segment has no uplift. Compare recorded `upliftPercent`, `upliftAmount`, `impliedUpliftPercent`, and `upliftMatches` only where their inputs are available. A dimension unit price can override the price book price for that segment; use `dimensionUnitPrice` when available. For discounts use each segment's recorded additional discount, and defer detailed current-segment discount arithmetic to the Discount Agent.

Time-based dimensions can be Year, Quarter, Month or Custom; a One-time dimension requires a time-based dimension and is not prorated. Full segments come first and a partial segment can appear at the end; use segment dates, `prorateMultiplier`, `summary.hasPartialSegment`, and the first segment term end date when available. Do not infer that a segment is partial from price alone. Renewal and amendment details require evidence beyond the segment prices; `line.renewedSubscriptionId` and `line.upgradedSubscriptionId` alone do not establish their calculation.
