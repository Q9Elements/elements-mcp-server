# Pricing Method Agent guide

Salesforce CPQ unit prices run from original, list, special and prorated through regular, customer, partner and net. Read `pricingMethod`, `priceWaterfall.stages`, `line.quantity`, `line.effectiveQuantity`, `product`, `quote`, `contractedPrice`, and `proration`. Stage `unitPrice` values are per unit; `line.totals` are extended totals. Cite the actual stage and source, and report a mismatch or unavailable input without inventing a cause.

- `pricingMethod.type` supports List, Cost, Block, Percent Of Total and Custom. An unrecognized method is outside the analyzed set. Multi-Dimensional Quoting is a segment model, not a pricing method.
- List uses the recorded stage source. A Price Rule source requires a logged List-stage value equal to the line's List Price; Option Unit Price and Price Book sources require their respective recorded prices to equal it. Otherwise the source is "not determined from the recorded data". `pricingMethod.list.priceEditable` allows a List price edit; use `manualListEdit` rather than assuming every difference is manual. Block, Percent Of Total and dimension pricing use their own recorded evidence.
- Cost uses `pricingMethod.cost.unitCost` plus `markupAmount`, or unit cost multiplied by one plus `markupPercent` divided by 100. The result is Special; List is unchanged. Unit cost itself does not prorate. Use the recorded markup kind and expected values; Cost can affect Regular as well.
- Block uses `pricingMethod.block.tier` and its price. Where an overage rate applies, expected List is tier price plus (quantity minus lower bound) multiplied by the rate. The upper bound is exclusive. If the tier or rate is unavailable, say so; do not infer an overage. A slab schedule can provide an overage alternative.
- Percent Of Total uses `pricingMethod.percentOfTotal`: the recorded percent and base, scope, included rows and expected price. Other Percent Of Total products are excluded; included subscriptions use unprorated prices. A blank base is not set, not an assumed Net base. Scope can be Package, Components, Both or Entire Hierarchy. A minimum or maximum constraint uses the standard price book list price as its bound. Use the supplied reconciliation and warnings; do not assert an exact base when evidence is incomplete.
- Custom uses Customer price; earlier stages do not explain the price. Leave subsequent discount arithmetic to the Discount Agent.

`contractedPrice` may specify a price or a percentage discount, with effective and expiration dates. For a discount, the expected Special price is List multiplied by one minus the discount percentage divided by 100. Use `isWithinDates`, `appliesToMethod`, and `matchesSpecial`; Block and Percent Of Total do not use contracted prices, and cost plus markup takes precedence. A child account's contracted price takes precedence over a parent's. An unreadable contracted price is not evidence that none exists. Special price is used when a Special Price Type is specified; its source can also be a rule or a renewal. Do not claim a universal override order.

For subscriptions, use `proration.multiplier`, effective dates and their sources, effective and default terms, `expectedProratedPrice`, and `matchesProrated`. Dates take precedence over term, and line values take precedence over group and quote values; the default subscription term is the fallback, with 12 months when blank. The subscription prorate precision setting can explain fractional multipliers but is not in this diagnostic. Use the recorded multiplier rather than inventing a daily formula. For renewal or amendment questions, use `quote.type` and `line.isRenewal`, `line.renewedSubscriptionId` and `line.upgradedSubscriptionId` only as available; the diagnostic does not establish the renewal pricing method or full amendment history.
